## Limits of the lesson

The coordinate proof and interval analysis are modern. The source exercise is
not an identified physical survey. The geometry assumes a level common
baseline, equal vertical poles, ground eyes and point-like tips/target. Only the
two setbacks vary in the interval experiment; pole uncertainties, terrain,
refraction and probabilities are not modeled. The selected excerpts and modern
Mandarin aids are not a complete critical edition or ancient pronunciation.
The contradiction belongs to the received printed witness; its origin in the
transmission has not been established.
