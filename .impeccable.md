# Design context

The creator requests distinctive ancient-world educational projects, source
languages and illustrations for fluent/native English readers. They explicitly
reject generic catalogue ideas and superficial work. They authorize sustained
solo implementation and pushing when ready.

Haidao applies that brief to the first sea-island surveying problem attributed
to Liu Hui, with its later commentary and editorial corrections. Learners work
through the numerical recipe, test a candidate against two observations, read
Classical Chinese with English aids, and inspect measurement uncertainty.

Design decisions for this project: a precise field notebook, generous readable
English and Chinese type, restrained cool paper and ink, and functional sight-line
colours. Show the short setback difference prominently. Preserve an equal-scale
whole-survey view; label any magnified diagram explicitly. Keep evidence and
operations together. Use actual whole source pages, without decorative ageing,
invented historical landscapes, generic cards or ornamental statistics.

The source case is an exercise, not a claimed historical field measurement.
Modern analysis, uncertainty intervals and comparison records are identified.
